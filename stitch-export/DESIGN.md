## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intellectual, highly crafted aesthetic tailored for an authoritative educational luminary. Borrowing rigor from architectural monographs and the digital mastery of award-winning cultural platforms, the UI elevates the learning experience from utilitarian course delivery into an evocative editorial journey. 

The emotional signature is commanding, thoughtful, and deeply human. It rejects generic modern SaaS patterns—such as bouncy drop shadows, hyper-saturated neon accents, and heavy pill containers—in favor of structural discipline, dramatic typography scale shifts, crisp hairline dividers, and deliberate interplay between dark scholarly depths and gallery-like ivory surfaces. Every screen feels curated like a high-end publication, instilling reverent focus, intellectual clarity, and institutional prestige.

## Colors

The palette leverages a deep oceanic foundation punctuated by academic architectural blues and tactile ivory surfaces.

- **Primary Dark Canvas (`#071B33` - Deep Navy):** The foundational void for main dark interfaces, providing visual gravity and focus.
- **Elevated Dark Surface (`#0B2545` - Midnight Blue):** Used for compartmentalized surface blocks, navigation bands, and deep architectural framing.
- **Primary Brand Accent (`#123B65` - Royal Navy):** The structural anchor. Applied to primary CTAs, active indicators, and structural hairline grids.
- **Secondary Accent (`#245B91` - Academic Blue):** Selected for text highlights, progress tracks, active interactive states, and metadata callouts.
- **Editorial Canvas (`#F7F6F2` - Soft Ivory):** Introduced in alternating editorial layout acts to inject deliberate high-contrast pacing. It serves as an intellectual reading canvas contrasting with the nocturnal deep navy sections.
- **Pristine Contrast (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Reserved for sharp display typography on dark canvases, iconography, and maximum emphasis.
- **Editorial Ink (`#172536`):** The primary reading ink applied to typography across ivory surfaces to preserve deep contrast without harsh true-black clipping.
- **Neutral Muted (`#64748B` - Slate):** Secondary narrative labels, footnotes, timestamps, and architectural secondary rules.

## Typography

The typographic doctrine strictly bans serif typefaces while retaining severe academic authority through scale, rhythm, and tension. 

- **Headlines (Syne):** Syne commands major narrative anchor points with sculptural, tight-tracked, geometric gravitas. Headings must always utilize tight tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.04em`) to forge a unified typographic block.
- **Body & Longform (Manrope):** Chosen for its balance of crisp geometric clarity and warm humanistic stroke terminals. Line heights remain generous (`1.6` to `1.8`) to prioritize effortless extended reading across both syllabus breakdowns and long-form transcripts.
- **Labels, Badges & Meta (Space Grotesk):** Provides a deliberate technical cadence. Rendered primarily in uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`0.05em` to `0.1em`) for syllabus indexes, chapter counters, course timecodes, and taxonomic filtering.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored in an asymmetrical, editorial 12-column grid that mirrors architectural drafting layouts. Rather than uniform container rows, layout sections flow with deliberate tension: offset text blocks, full-bleed imagery bordered by fine rules, and dramatic shifts in negative space.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with 2rem gutters and generous 4rem minimum outer canvas margins. Master modules use intentional column offsets (e.g., a 4-column master thesis quote placed next to a 7-column dense syllabus grid).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with 1.5rem gutters and 2.5rem canvas margins. Content compresses rhythmically, shifting secondary metadata alongside rather than underneath primary titles.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4 columns with 1rem gutters and 1.25rem margins. Asymmetrical offsets collapse into stacked architectural modules separated by precise horizontal rules.
- **Rhythm & Flow:** Large thematic sections maintain vertical separation using `space-xl` combined with 1px border dividers to denote progression between lesson chapters and conceptual shifts.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly **Sharp (`0px`)**. 

All elements—buttons, cards, video players, badges, tab navigations, and dialogs—use unrounded, architectural corners. This eliminates the juvenile softness typical of mainstream software and aligns the interface with physical broadsheet newspapers, mathematical treatises, and mid-century editorial design.

Small micro-notches, diagonal chamfers (45-degree corner cutouts of 6px on badges), and thin intersecting wireframe accents are permitted to reinforce the technical and scholarly drafting motif.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Royal Navy (`#123B65`) fill, pure white text in `label-lg`, sharp 0px corners, 1px border in `#245B91`. Padding: 1rem horizontal, 0.875rem vertical. On hover, background shifts to Academic Blue (`#245B91`) with a 3px right-down hard shadow in `#F7F6F2` or `#FFFFFF`.
- **Editorial Ghost Action:** Transparent background with a 1px crisp rule in `#245B91` (or `#172536` on ivory surfaces). Text in Space Grotesk. Hover triggers a solid fill inversion with instantaneous state transitions (0.15s linear).
- **Text Link CTA:** Underlined with an offset 1px rule, accompanied by a directional glyph (`->`) using Space Grotesk with an exaggerated hover translate effect (`transform: translateX(4px)`).

### Cards & Content Modules
- Rejects generic floating white cards. Modules are constructed as structural structural boxes defined by 1px rules (`rgba(18, 59, 101, 0.35)` on dark, `rgba(23, 37, 54, 0.1)` on light).
- Each card incorporates an index header (e.g., `VOL. 01 / MODULE 04`) styled in `label-sm` with Space Grotesk, positioned flush against a top horizontal dividing hairline.

### Chips & Badges
- Strict rectangular forms (no pill shapes).
- Composed of a 1px border in Academic Blue (`#245B91`), uppercase text in `label-sm`, and an optional miniature geometric accent (e.g., a 4px solid square indicator).
- Background is either transparent or a 10% tint of Academic Blue.

### Lists & Syllabus Breakdowns
- Numbered with leading zeros (`01`, `02`, `03`) set in oversized Syne typography (`headline-md`) rendered in Slate (`#64748B`).
- List items are separated by full-width 1px hairline horizontal dividers. On hover, the list row responds with an ambient background shift to `#0B2545` and an active indicator mark on the left edge.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Strictly square geometries for both checkboxes and radio buttons.
- Unchecked: 1px border in `#64748B` over `#071B33`.
- Checked: Solid fill in `#123B65` framing an inner micro-square in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).

### Input Fields
- Understated architectural inputs. Transparent background bounded by a crisp bottom 1px rule (or full 1px border) in `#245B91/50`.
- Placeholder text set in Manrope (`body-md`) in muted Slate. On focus, the bottom border shifts to Pure White with a 1px upward offset accent in Academic Blue. No glowing outer rings.

### Specialized Editorial Components
- **Marginalia / Side Annotations:** Narrow, 2-column wide side notes running parallel to long-form reading segments, rendered in Space Grotesk (`body-sm`) in `#64748B` to emulate scholarly footnoting.
- **Architectural Media Frame:** Lecture video displays and masterclass visual assets sit flush inside an explicit hairline grid frame with coordinate indicators (`LAT / LNG` or `00:00:00:00` SMPTE timecode markings) pinned to outer corners.
